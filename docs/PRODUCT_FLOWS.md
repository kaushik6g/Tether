# Tether product flows

## Mutual tap and chat

1. A user taps the heart next to a friend in their private circle.
2. The tap is stored privately. The friend receives no notification and sees no pending state.
3. If the friend later taps back, the server creates one match for the pair.
4. Both people receive a match notification and can open their private chat.
5. Tether suggests a plan such as “Coffee Thursday at 5?” Either person can accept or suggest another plan.

The match notification should say both people want to catch up. It should say they are both free only when availability has actually been shared.

## Browser invitation

1. A user chooses a friend who is not on Tether and reviews the invite message.
2. The user sends a short HTTPS link through their preferred messaging app.
3. The recipient opens a lightweight browser page that explains who invited them and why.
4. The recipient can install Tether and continue from the invite link, or close the page.
5. Opening or accepting an invite does not create a mutual match. The recipient chooses whether to join and whether to add the sender to their circle.

An invitation deliberately tells the recipient that the sender is thinking of them. This is separate from a private heart tap, which stays hidden unless it becomes mutual.
